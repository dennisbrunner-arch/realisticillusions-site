About

Our story

When I was young, I always wanted to be able to draw. With lots of erasing and concentration I could make semi-respectable artwork, but I was jealous of people who could do it with ease. It wasn't until a few decades later that I found I could work in three dimensions much more easily, recreating things in clay, foam and other materials. I just needed that extra dimension.

Today Realistic Illusions LLC works out of a shop outside Cheyenne, Wyoming, building for theatres, museums, businesses, churches, collectors and homeowners across the Colorado Front Range.
